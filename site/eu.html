Sources

Every number links back to the original record.

VotesRoll-call results from HowTheyVote.eu open data (based on the Parliament's official minutes), data as of . Each vote in an MEP's card links to its HowTheyVote page and the officially adopted text.
Which votes countFinal votes this term about Ukraine and Russia, chosen by this site (the list is in every MEP's card). “Proposals to reject” Ukraine aid or the Russian gas ban count in reverse: voting FOR them is anti-Ukraine.
ScoreSame formula as the US page: weighted share of pro- vs anti-Ukraine votes (weights 0.25–1.25 by importance). Abstentions and absences are not counted; MEPs with a short record are pulled toward their group's average.
MEPsMembership, groups and national parties from HowTheyVote and the European Parliament's MEP directory.
Only the European Parliament is shown: it is the one EU institution whose members are directly elected. The Council and European Council are made up of national governments, and the Commission is appointed.
